Left It Here keeps a huge note of where the keys, glasses, remote, wallet, cane, and book were put. Kids can type the place. Grandparents can open Look and read it from across the room.

Each place stays on this phone. Clear one when you move the thing, or leave the app and come back.

Left It Here does not use an account, ads, or a network connection. It is a home note for things you already own.
